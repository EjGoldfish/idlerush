What is IdleRush?

IdleRush is a free, browser-based gaming platform focused on idle, clicker, arcade, and casual games. Every game is hand-picked or purpose-built to work perfectly in any modern browser — no plugins, no downloads, no logins required. We believe the best games are the ones you can jump into instantly.

🚀
Instant Play
Every game on IdleRush loads directly in your browser. There's nothing to install, no account to create, and no waiting. Click play and you're in — on desktop, tablet, or mobile.
🎮
Idle & Clicker Focus
We specialize in idle and incremental games — the kind where your empire grows even when you step away. From cookie clickers to tycoon builders, our library is built for satisfying progression loops.
🔒
Your Progress, Saved
Game saves are stored locally in your browser so your progress is always there when you come back. No cloud sync needed — your data stays on your device.
🆕
Always Growing
New games are added to IdleRush regularly. We build original titles and curate the best free browser experiences. Bookmark the site and check back — there's always something new to try.

How It Works

Getting started on IdleRush takes about three seconds. Here's all there is to it:

01
Browse the Library
Scroll through the game grid or filter by category — Idle, Clicker, Arcade, Puzzle — or search by name to find exactly what you're after.
02
Hit Play
Click the Play button on any game card. The game loads instantly in your browser tab. No download prompts, no login screens — it just works.
03
Save & Come Back
Your progress is automatically saved to your browser's local storage. Close the tab, return later, and pick up right where you left off.
04
Check for New Titles
We add new games regularly. Bookmark IdleRush and check back often — the library is always growing with fresh idle, clicker, and arcade titles.
